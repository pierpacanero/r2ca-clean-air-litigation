    About the project

    The Clean Air Litigation Database is developed within R2CA — The Right to Clean Air, a research project of the University of Turin. The database collects judicial decisions and pending proceedings in which the quality of the air people breathe is at stake — before domestic courts, the Court of Justice of the European Union and the European Court of Human Rights.

    Each case is presented through an original abstract drafted and validated by the project's editorial team, together with the essential procedural data and links to the official sources of the decisions.

    Methodology

    • Unit of the database. The unit is the case, understood as a single dispute: a case may span several proceedings and instances (first instance, appeal, preliminary references, enforcement), which are listed within the same record. Pending proceedings are included and identified by their status.
    • Geography and forum. Each case is assigned to the country where the facts arose; the deciding authority is recorded separately, so that decisions of international courts remain findable both from the country concerned and from the dedicated international-courts entry point. No sub-national breakdown is applied.
    • Abstracts. All abstracts are original texts of the editorial team; they summarise, and do not replace, the decisions they describe.
    • Sources. Wherever available, records link to the official text of each decision or to established repositories, including the Sabin Center's Climate Change Litigation Databases.
    • Boundaries. The map is a navigation aid only: the depiction of borders and territories implies no position on questions of sovereignty.

    How to cite

    R2CA — The Right to Clean Air, Clean Air Litigation Database, University of Turin, entry «[case name]», last updated [date].
